# Critic rubric (from the reference workflow; score each 0-10, give the exact second of every lost point)
Reference: the user's reference shorts (2D-style characters in a 3D world, millions of views).
1. Character acting: 2+ expression/pose changes per second on screen, holds of 0.4 s or more, no stiff bodies.
2. Face size: heads big enough to read the expression (target: head >= 30% of frame height most of the time).
3. Sharpness: crisp edges and text, no smeared or shimmering surfaces.
4. Beat on screen: every narrated beat is shown, not just said.
5. World richness: 5+ distinct places that look reasonable (nothing floating, buried, inside walls or on water).
6. Grade: bright, saturated, pleasing, close to the references; no washed-out or muddy frames.
7. Comedy timing: a 0.5-1 s hold after each gag/reveal.
8. Camera variety: 3+ kinds of angle, motivated moves, no jarring hard cuts.
9. Captions: large, 1-3 words, readable, sitting in the lower third around 68-72% of the height.
10. Sound sync: a sound effect on every hit/impact/reveal, within +/-2 frames, not buried under the voice.
Also judge: is the hook instant, does the story escalate, is the twist clear, would a viewer rewatch (loop)?
