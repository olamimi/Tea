# The Kid Who Built a Wheel - 90 s script (draft v3)
Target ~330 words at the narrator's measured pace (3.8 words/s incl. pauses) = ~88-92 s.
Camera: one continuous move per scene; zoom-through between scenes; whip+blur only at LAUNCH and CRASH; 3 hard cuts (marked CUT).

1. HOOK (wheel, slow orbit)
In this world, this is the most illegal object on Earth.

2. NO WHEELS (busy street, camera glides along a river of porters)
Because nobody ever invented the wheel. So everything is carried. Your groceries. Your sofa. Your piano. Your grandpa. Carrying is the biggest job on the planet. Half the world are porters, and the other half pays them.

3. THE KID (street corner, push in on a small kid crushed under a huge crate)
And one kid was really, really tired of carrying.

4. THE SHED (workshop, camera drifts over the failed attempts)
So he started building. He tried a square. It did not roll. He tried a triangle. It rolled once, and it hurt. Attempt number forty-seven... was round.

5. THE CART (street, tracking alongside)
He put four of them under a box, and moved his whole house in one trip. A job that takes ten porters, done by one kid, before breakfast. CUT -> the ten porters frozen, crates dropping.

6. THE GUILD (hall, slow push toward the stage)
The porters' guild called an emergency meeting. If wheels caught on, half the world would lose their jobs. Pianos would roll. Grandpas would roll. Nobody would ever need to be carried again. The boss went red.

7. THE BOUNTY (poster)
So by lunch, the guild had a price on his head. CUT -> WANTED poster: one thousand gold, for the kid who built a wheel.

8. THE CHASE (hill top, crowd closing in)
Porters came from every street. They were strong, they were angry, and there were a lot of them. So the kid did the only thing he could. WHIP -> He rolled.

9. THE HILL (downhill, camera racing alongside)
Straight down the steepest hill in the city, faster than anything had ever moved. Porters can carry a piano up a mountain, but nobody can catch a wheel going downhill.

10. THE CRASH (lobby)
At the bottom was the guild boss's ground-floor penthouse. WHIP -> And its very large window.

11. THE TWIST (lobby, slow push on the boss)
The boss stood up. He looked at the kid. He looked at the wheel. And he bought it. Not to use it. To hide it. CUT -> Now he owns the only wheel on Earth, and uses it as a coffee table.
