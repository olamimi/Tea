# "In this world, the wheel was never invented" — script draft (~42 s)

| # | Beat | Narration | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook (0-3 s) | "In this world, the penthouse is on the ground floor." | Rich man lounging in a gold lobby at street level; zoom-through the window |
| 2 | The rule | "Because here, nobody ever invented the wheel." | A caveman rolls a round stone... a second caveman stops it with his foot. Colour-flash |
| 3 | Consequence 1 | "No carts. No cars. Everything you buy arrives on someone's back." | Street of porters: a piano, a sofa, a fridge, all carried; whip pan |
| 4 | Consequence 2 | "So every city grew along a river. Boats were the only fast lane." | Canal city, boat traffic jam, a gondola "taxi" |
| 5 | Consequence 3 | "No wheel means no pulley. No pulley, no elevator." | Tower with only stairs; a sweaty office worker on floor 40 |
| 6 | Midpoint spectacle | "So the higher you live, the more you climb. The poor live at the top." | Spinning pull-back over a skyline of towers wrapped in staircases |
| 7 | Consequence 4 | "No gears, so no clocks. Here, being late is measured in shadows." | Giant sundial in a square; a boss tapping it |
| 8 | Twist | "Every year, someone invents the wheel again. And every year, the porters vote it out." | Inventor with a wheel; a crowd of porters with "NO WHEELS" signs |
| 9 | Loop ending | "Which is exactly why, in this world..." | Push back into the gold ground-floor lobby; flows into the opening line |

Camera: never still; swoops within places, whip pans and zoom-throughs between them, one spinning pull-back at the midpoint, one colour flash. No dissolves; at most one hard cut.
