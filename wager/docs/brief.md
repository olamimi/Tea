# IN THIS WORLD — "The Burger Wager"

**Format:** "In this world…" vertical Short (1080×1920, 30 fps)
**Length:** 1:47
**Style:** 2D cut-out puppet characters in toon-shaded 3D sets, plum outlines, soft depth of field, bloom, and big two-word captions
**Logline:** In a world where everyone bets on God because the math says they should, the last skeptic finally gives in. Then he dies and finds out the real God punishes exactly that kind of bet.

---

## 1. The idea

- **Hook:** "In this world, everybody believes in God", followed immediately by the trending panicked **"huss… huss… huss…"** breathing.
- **Setup:** no evidence for God and no evidence against. Philosophers break the tie with the **Burger Wager** (a parody of Pascal's Wager): believe and you lose nothing at worst and win infinite heaven at best. Don't believe and you risk infinite fire.
- **Conflict:** the narrator is the last non-believer on Earth. His argument is that you can't switch belief on because someone offers you a prize. Family pressure and the fear of burning wear him down, the math of infinity beats him, and he converts.
- **False happy ending:** church life, purpose, casseroles. Sixty years pass and he dies.
- **Twist:** he wakes in a white room and celebrates, until he sees everyone being thrown into the fire. God hid on purpose and rewards **warranted** belief, not bets. The wager was never a two-option bet. There were infinite possible gods, and they only thought of one.
- **Button:** in hell, deadpan: *"And the worst part? I was right the first time."*

## 3. Character design

All characters share the house style: big round white head, small dot eyes, thick plum outlines (#301420), a bean-shaped body, stick limbs with round white "glove" hands, cel shading with a soft rim light, and expressive brows that carry most of the acting.

### The Narrator (young): the skeptic
- **Look:** messy brown curls (#6b3f22), round wire glasses, blue hoodie (#3f6fd6) with a darker pouch and white drawstrings.
- **Personality:** dry, stubborn, logical. Default face: stern brows, half-lidded eyes, flat mouth.
- **Signature poses:** arms folded among kneeling crowds, hand on chin, deadpan to camera.

### The Narrator (60 years later)
- **Look:** the same face with grey-white curls, glasses, a grey walrus moustache and light wrinkles. Brown cardigan over a cream shirt.
- **Arc:** content believer, then overjoyed in the white room, then horrified, then deadpan in hell.

### Mom
- **Look:** long dark-brown hair with bangs, lashes, pink cardigan, small gold pendant.
- **Personality:** loving, worried, prays constantly. Acting: clasped hands, worried brows, welling tears.

### Bro (best friend)
- **Look:** backwards red cap, red jersey with "#23".
- **Personality:** blunt, loyal, says the quiet part out loud ("Literally.").

### The Pastor
- **Look:** shiny bald dome with grey side hair, black shirt with a white clerical collar tab.
- **Personality:** warm and cheerful. Carries a book or a baby.

### The Philosopher (inventor of the Burger Wager)
- **Look:** shiny bald head with a laurel wreath, huge white beard and curled moustache (no visible mouth), white chiton with a red sash and gold trim.
- **Personality:** smug genius. Eureka finger, chef's kiss.

### The Rival Philosopher
- **Look:** brown beard, lavender-white robe with a blue sash.
- **Role:** the other half of the "Total stalemate."

### The Scientist
- **Look:** wild white tufts of hair, big round glasses, white lab coat with a blue tie, pocket pens, clipboard.
- **Role:** telescope, microscope, meteorite-licker, deadpan "Nothing."

### Townsfolk (crowds)
- Red-cap moustache man, stubbly guy with glasses in yellow, purple woman with an afro, green woman with a pink bob. Used for kneeling crowds, the casserole congregation and the people falling into the fire.

### God
- **Never shown as a character.** A giant glowing orb of white-gold light with slowly rotating rays. It pulses gently in time with each spoken word.

## 5. Voice and narration

| Role | Engine / voice | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| Narrator | Chatterbox (ResembleAI, run locally), default voice | exaggeration 0.65, cfg 0.35, temperature 0.8. Sped up to **1.1×** (tempo only), then **pitched up 2 semitones**. |
| "Huss… huss… huss…" | Chatterbox | exaggeration 0.9 (more breathy panic), played **1.8× faster** so it's quick and frantic |
| Mom | Kokoro, voice `af_heart` | speed 0.95, then 1.1× tempo. Warm, slightly teary. No pitch shift. |
| Bro | Kokoro, voice `am_puck` | speed 1.05, then 1.1× tempo. Casual, blunt. |
| God | Kokoro, voice `bm_george` | speed 0.82, **pitched down to 0.8×** with formant preserved, plus a large echo (60 ms / 140 ms) and a +4 dB bass boost. No speed-up. Slow, huge, calm. |

**Delivery notes**
- The narrator is dry and conversational, with punchy pauses before reveals ("They called it… the Burger Wager").
- Extra silence around the big beats: before the Burger Wager, after "I died" (0.7 s), around God's lines, and before "We all burned" (0.6 s).
- Every take was checked with Whisper speech-to-text and re-generated when the words came out wrong.
- **Overall pace:** about 200 words per minute. The voice ends at 1:45.6 and the video at 1:47.4.

## 7. Motion notes (what keeps it fluid)

- Every shot has one clear action with eased motion: walk-ins, leaning in, kneeling down, handing things over, jumping, falling.
- Idle motion is slow and subtle: gentle sway, breathing and blinks. Nothing twitches on every word.
- The camera always moves slowly through a shot (push in, crane or pull back), with a very light handheld drift.
- Reactions use springy overshoot: eureka finger, arms up, shrug.
