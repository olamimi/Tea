# Style decisions

## Character design: keep our own (do NOT apply the Style Bible / style file character rules)
The user asked to disregard all character-design guidance from the "Explainer Style Bible" and the
"cartoon-explainer-style" notes. Characters keep the design built from the user's reference videos
(see motion-study.md): white heads with a consistent ink outline, pill torsos, stick limbs, dot hands,
low-set faces, hats/hair as 3D props. Not adopted: brown variable-width brush ink, rim halo,
squarish oval heads, 33-45% head size, warm off-white faces, single cel tone, changed proportions.

## Still taken from the Style Bible (non-character)
- Warm field with teal accent; colour grade per story beat (greyer at setbacks, golden at insight, cool at consequence)
- Light landing in scenes (window/blind light), depth of field, warm grade + grain + vignette
- Camera never still; eased push-ins; whip pans / zoom-throughs preferred over hard cuts
- Story structure (hook, open question, physical reveal, loop ending); props that repeat the theme
- Audio around -15 LUFS with a narrow loudness range

## Open decisions
- Captions: one word mid-screen vs 1-3 words in the lower third (current)
